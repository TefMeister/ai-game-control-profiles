# Far Cry 3 Blood Dragon: two input traps (2026-10-11, /lm, dev PC)

For `profiles/far-cry-3-blood-dragon.json`. `[verified-live 2026-10-11, n=1 each]`

- A tutorial box ("JUMP ... OK") can appear early on foot and swallows the next Escape; press Enter to close it first.
- Skipping the story clips with Enter + Escape: one pair too many lands in gameplay and opens the PAUSE menu (then the
  servers alert, ~30 s, and its ERROR box). Look before each pair.
- Main menu after launch: the online ERROR box is up; Enter, Enter (Campaign), CONTINUE highlighted, Enter; the save
  loads in ~35 s into a story clip.
