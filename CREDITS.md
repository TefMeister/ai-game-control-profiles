# Credits & Attribution

## Get credited, or ask us to stop — read this first

**If you should be credited here and are not, or are credited incorrectly, email
**td3kxlvr@proton.me** and we will fix it as soon as possible, no argument and no
questions asked.** That applies whether your work reached us as a tool, a technique, a
forum post, a wiki edit, a video, or an idea we absorbed without noticing where it came
from. We would rather be told than quietly get it wrong.

We also honour correction and removal requests from the actual rights holders of anything
used here, promptly, via the same address.

This notice is deliberately at the top rather than buried at the bottom, because the list
below is genuinely incomplete and we know it.

## Why this file is short

This repository is **data, not code** — plain JSON profiles and plain-English conventions
describing how to operate a game programmatically. Its contents were produced by our own
hands-on reverse-engineering of the games listed, in our own repositories, rather than
assembled from anyone else's published work.

So there is no long dependency list to credit here, and we have deliberately **not** filled
this file with plausible-sounding names we cannot actually verify contributed. An invented
credit is worse than a missing one: it is wrong about a real person.

## What the profiles were derived from

| Profile | Derived from |

Both games' own developers and publishers made the works being described. We are only
documenting how to drive them; we did not make them, and all rights to those games and
their assets belong to their owners. **No game files, assets, or code are included here.**

## The wider debt

The methods behind these profiles — DLL proxying, hooking, input injection, memory
inspection — are decades of accumulated community knowledge from modding forums, wikis,
write-ups, and open-source tools, most of it authored by people whose names never got
attached to the technique. The per-game credit files in our other repositories name
everyone we could actually identify for that game. This one names the games, and this
paragraph names the debt we cannot itemise.

If you recognise your own work in here, the section at the top of this file is for you.
